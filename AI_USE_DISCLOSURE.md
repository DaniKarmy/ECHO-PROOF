# AI Use Disclosure

ECHO PROOF was developed through documented human–AI collaboration.

## Human contribution — Daniela

Daniela originated and shaped the project direction, made final project and scope decisions, reviewed scientific/claim boundaries, supplied the decisive human perceptual tests, selected the final Matched A/B presentation after testing, reviewed the application visually and functionally on the target laptop, and retains final authority over release and submission.

## AI assistance — ChatGPT / LK

ChatGPT assisted with research and rule verification, deterministic mapping design, code generation and debugging, local test/verification scripts, release/security review, evidence organization, source/provenance checks, and documentation drafting.

Material outputs were not accepted automatically. Claims, mappings, runtime behavior and project decisions were tested, challenged, revised or rejected when evidence required it.

The browser application itself does **not** call an AI/LLM at runtime. It contains no model/API dependency; the sonification is deterministic JavaScript/Web Audio logic.
